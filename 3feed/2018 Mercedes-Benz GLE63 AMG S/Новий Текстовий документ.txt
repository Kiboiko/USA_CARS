This 2018 Mercedes-Benz GLE63 AMG S features a Black exterior over a Black interior and is powered by a handcrafted 577-horsepower twin-turbocharged 5.5-liter V8 engine paired with an automatic transmission and AMG Performance 4MATIC all-wheel drive. As a high-performance utility vehicle, it utilizes a performance-tuned suspension and advanced drivetrain calibration to manage its substantial power, ensuring stability under acceleration, strong midrange torque, and refined high-speed capability. The exterior highlights a muscular yet restrained luxury-SUV profile, while the cabin provides premium materials, supportive seating, and a sophisticated technology interface designed for daily usability. Ultimately, this configuration functions as a highly versatile and practical package that effectively integrates premium, all-season comfort with the distinctive driving dynamics of a high-output AMG V8 platform.
