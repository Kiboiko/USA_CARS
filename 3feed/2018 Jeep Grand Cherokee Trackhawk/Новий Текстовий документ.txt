This 2018 Jeep Grand Cherokee Trackhawk features a Billet Silver exterior over a black interior and is powered by a 707-horsepower supercharged 6.2-liter V8 engine paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission and a performance-oriented all-wheel-drive system. To manage this high level of output while maintaining daily utility, the platform utilizes an adaptive suspension strategy, specialized chassis calibration, and a high-capacity Brembo braking system, with this specific example incorporating an AFE cold-air intake and a resonator delete for a more pronounced induction and exhaust character. The exterior presents an understated but purposeful performance profile, while the cabin focuses on occupant comfort, supportive seating, and modern infotainment integration. Ultimately, this configuration functions as a highly capable utility vehicle that effectively pairs exceptional straight-line speed and all-weather traction with premium family practicality.
