This 2018 BMW M3 Competition features a Mineral Gray exterior over a black leather interior and is powered by a 444-horsepower twin-turbocharged 3.0-liter inline-six engine paired with a rapid-shifting automatic performance transmission. The factory Competition package sharpens the rear-wheel-drive sedan’s dynamics with focused chassis tuning, enhanced steering calibration, and an electronically controlled rear differential that works alongside the suspension to optimize traction, corner-exit stability, and driver engagement. The exterior combines understated executive proportions with aggressive, M-specific aerodynamics and a wide stance, while the cockpit maintains a driver-focused layout equipped with supportive sport seating designed around high-performance driving. Ultimately, this configuration functions as a highly precise and balanced package that effectively delivers modern, turbocharged inline-six performance within a practical four-door format.
