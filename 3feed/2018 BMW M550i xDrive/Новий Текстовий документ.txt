This 2018 BMW M550i xDrive features a Dark Graphite Metallic exterior over a black leather interior and is powered by a 456-horsepower twin-turbocharged 4.4-liter V8 engine paired with an automatic transmission and xDrive all-wheel drive. Positioned to balance executive refinement with serious performance capability, the chassis is tuned for high-speed stability and responsive handling, utilizing the all-wheel-drive system to deliver effortless torque with impressive all-weather composure. The exterior presents an understated sport-sedan profile, while the cabin offers premium materials, supportive seating, and a technology-rich environment designed for both long-distance highway comfort and more spirited driving. Ultimately, this configuration functions as a highly capable and versatile sport sedan that effectively combines discreet styling and everyday luxury with strong V8 performance.
