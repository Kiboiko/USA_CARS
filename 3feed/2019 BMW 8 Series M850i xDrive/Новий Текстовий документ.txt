This 2019 BMW 8 Series M850i xDrive convertible features a blue exterior over a brown interior and is powered by a 523-horsepower twin-turbocharged 4.4-liter V8 engine paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission and xDrive all-wheel drive. Designed as a high-performance grand touring model, the vehicle utilizes a rigid chassis, adaptive suspension control, and precise drivetrain calibration to deliver effortless V8 torque alongside refined high-speed stability and year-round composure. The exterior balances elegant flagship proportions with assertive sport details, while the cabin incorporates premium materials, advanced digital interfaces, and supportive seating engineered specifically for long-distance comfort. Ultimately, this configuration functions as a highly sophisticated and versatile package that effectively combines modern V8 performance with the upscale functionality of an open-air grand tourer.
